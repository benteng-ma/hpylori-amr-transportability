# Four-domain transport-shift expansion

Date: 2026-08-31

## Scope and lock

These analyses were added after the frozen clarithromycin and levofloxacin benchmark had been classified. They explain failure modes but cannot add markers, refit a catalogue, change an AST label, alter a diagnostic denominator, or revise the frozen transportability labels.

## Added analyses

1. Analytic availability: cohort- and drug-specific target callability.
2. Population shift: five-fold stratified near-clone-grouped L2-logistic discrimination of HpGP versus each external cohort using phenotype-blind core-SNP PC1-PC10; 2,000 group-bootstrap AUC intervals and 1,000 label permutations.
3. Conditional transport: exact lineage-specific sensitivity, specificity, false-susceptible rate, and resistance among marker-negative isolates.
4. Case-mix standardization: HpGP sensitivity and specificity directly standardized to each external cohort's phenotype-specific fixed-lineage distribution.
5. Clinical severity: Ningxia resistant isolates summarized across lower-bound MIC thresholds.
6. Per-isolate audit atlas: phenotype, frozen prediction, source-reported genotype, source-versus-recall agreement, MIC, development-manifold support, and full-*gyrA* missense burden.

## Principal results

- PC-only out-of-fold cohort-discriminator AUC was 0.899 (group-bootstrap 95% interval 0.871-0.924) for HpGP versus Ningxia and 0.918 (0.893-0.941) for HpGP versus the read cohort; both permutation P values were 0.001.
- Within dominant SNP cluster 3, Ningxia levofloxacin sensitivity was 12/23 (52.2%) versus 22/22 (100%) in HpGP (Fisher P=0.00020). Specificity was 22/29 (75.9%) versus 38/38 (100%; P=0.0018).
- Among frozen-marker-negative cluster-3 isolates, 11/33 Ningxia isolates were resistant versus 0/38 HpGP isolates (P=0.000076) and 2/23 read-cohort isolates (Ningxia comparison P=0.052).
- HpGP directly standardized to the Ningxia phenotype-specific lineage distribution retained 100% sensitivity and specificity, compared with observed Ningxia values of 42.9% and 75.9%. Marginal lineage composition therefore did not reproduce the performance loss.
- The 16 Ningxia false-susceptible isolates had a median lower-bound MIC of 16 mg/L (interquartile range 3.5-32); 11/16 were at least 8 mg/L and six were right-censored above 32 mg/L.

## Interpretation boundary

Local development-manifold proximity and learnable cohort-level covariate shift can coexist. The within-cluster comparison and standardization indicate a study-conditioned genotype-phenotype association shift, but study, assay, sequence snapshot, and lineage remain partially confounded. The analyses do not establish a causal lineage mechanism or identify a new resistance determinant.
