Proceed with the frozen clarithromycin/levofloxacin transportability benchmark using HpGP 414 as the catalogue/global internal cohort, Ningxia 60 as the primary MIC-linked external cohort, and Zenodo 52 as a secondary categorical/read-linked phenotype-method stress test.

This is conditional because both external cohorts are Chinese, Zenodo uses internally inconsistent custom disk-diffusion endpoints, HpGP has five unmatched phenotype IDs, and several large published cohorts lack a public isolate-level AST crosswalk. Those cohorts must not be counted as validation data merely because their assemblies are public.

Full acquisition and prespecified Phase 1 processing are permitted. Performance-driven thresholds, final Results prose, and broad geographic claims remain locked.
